Meet Agent

Agent is an AI assistant that takes real actions for you. It can write code, send email, organize files, schedule things, and browse the web. You delegate a task; Agent does it.

About this study

You'll use Agent to complete a series of short tasks our research team has prepared, to see how the agent handles a range of common requests. For each one, make the decisions you would with your own accounts and data.

How it works
  • You'll complete 10 short tasks with Agent (about 10 to 15 minutes).
  • The tasks are pre-written so everyone sees the same ones. Each appears in a pop-up; click "Send" to give it to Agent. There's no chat input.
  • At some points, Agent may ask you to confirm a step before continuing. Approving and declining are both normal responses, and the session continues either way. Respond as you naturally would.
